On Saturday, October 24, 2026, Richard took 1st place in Men's Masters Professional Bodybuilding at the WNBF Midwest Natural Nationals in Liberty, Missouri. It is a national title, and one he earned under circumstances few competitors ever face.
 
## A Difficult Year
 
2026 has been one of the hardest years of Richard's life. Early in the year, he developed significant brain inflammation that brought extreme brain fog, blurred and double vision, and other neurological problems. Every day he went to work, he came home with hives. His condition became so debilitating that he could not perform simple tasks.
 
Further evaluation pointed to exposure to biotoxins, believed to be mold, at his place of employment. Combined with exposure to electronics (EMFs), this led to severe brain inflammation that significantly affected his vision and damaged his neurological cell membranes. He was diagnosed with Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (CIRS) and Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (Myalgic Encephalomyelitis), and he also suffers from mitochondrial damage and dysfunction, which affects his energy levels. His condition was serious enough that he had to resign from his job.
 
Richard is now under the care of a medical doctor who specializes in brain injury and trauma as well as autoimmune disease. He has been in aggressive treatment for the past six to seven months, and his doctor expects he will need several years of continued treatment given the severity of his condition.
 
## Competing Anyway
 
Just when he was preparing to step on stage, the challenges continued. During the week of the competition, Richard had a brief exposure to mold and broke out in hives, along with numerous other unpleasant symptoms.
 
He competed anyway.

Richard's win is a testament to discipline, resilience, and refusing to quit when things get hard. If you're facing a health battle or a setback of your own, let his story serve as a reminder that progress is possible, even when the odds seem stacked against you.
